Vegetable Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, sliced
  • 2 stalks celery, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 4 to 5 cups Vegetable broth or water
  • 1.5 cups Mixed frozen vegetables
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon dried Herbs
Category Servings Prep Time
dinner 4 30 min

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot and sauté the chopped garlic and onions for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the carrots, celery, potatoes, diced tomatoes, and broth, bring to a boil, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the mixed frozen vegetables, salt, pepper, and herbs, then simmer for another 5 minutes until tender.
